| Nov 5, 2026 | **Pawn loans:** 30-day term at 10% per 30 days, prorated by days held with a 7-day minimum, amounts rounded up to 10 ₽. 3 days' grace (interest keeps running), then the item is forfeited to the safe at paid = principal. Pawners ask 0.6 × what they'd ask to sell. About 29% of walk-ins want a loan (more if desperate or heirloom). Redeem chance falls with desperation and rises for heirlooms; people who pawned a fake come back about 0.2× as often, so a fake pawn is how you get stuck with one. A redeemer who gives up waiting comes back the next day. |

| Nov 5, 2026 | **Specialists (M2):** Faina Gelfand, jeweler (gold, coins: 6,000 ₽/month, 6 calls, 1,500 ₽ per extra call; accurate but lowballs good pieces), and Ilya Semyonovich Brodsky, watchmaker (watches: 7,500 ₽/month, 5 calls, 2,000 ₽ extra; honest, failing eyesight). | Nov 5, 2026 | **Selling (M2):** buyers are planned at day open from displayed, tagged items: daily chance = base 0.28 × price factor × area visibility (counter 1, wall 0.6, back 0.3) × case bonus × demand × reputation, at most 4 buyers a day. Their hidden ceiling comes from true value × demand × temperament, never above your tag; naive buyers can be partly fooled by fakes. Holding firm sells slowly for more. The dealer stays as a fallback at 58% of value. The shop sheet never shows true value; price chips are multiples of what you paid. |
| Nov 5, 2026 | **Demand and news (M2):** season tables per tag, flat holiday windows (New Year, Defender Day, Women's Day, Victory Day, White Nights, school start), a seeded mean-reverting drift per tag (0.8–1.25) with occasional shocks (ruble wobble, phone launch…). The morning paper (*Вечерний Мостоград*) runs 1–3 stories that hint at demand without numbers. |

| Nov 5, 2026 | **M2 simulator result (`npm run sim`, 40 seeds):** careful policy (checks every item, passes on red flags, opens at 40% of its estimate, keeps a 10-day rent reserve, tags at ~1.05× its estimate) grows ~18%/month over 60 and 90 days and never goes under; careless policy (one loupe look, offers near the claim, tags at cost, dumps stock on the dealer) is insolvent by day 29 in every run. `tests/sim.test.ts` guards the bands (careful 8–30%/month, careless bust ≤ day 40). "Bust" = worth (cash + stock at cost + loans out) below zero. |
| Nov 5, 2026 | **Bake pipeline as built (M3 task 1):** Blender 4.5.15 LTS (pinned), geometry generated by script from `bake/layout.json` into one mesh per material kind (baked / dynamic / glass / unlit, set in `bake/materials.py`). Lightmap = Cycles diffuse direct+indirect without colour on uv1, OIDN-denoised, 8-bit sRGB PNG ÷ `lightmapScale`, shipped as KTX2 UASTC (+RDO, Zstd) via the `ktx2-encoder` npm package instead of `toktx`, so CI installs no system tools. Lightmapped surfaces render as `MeshBasicMaterial` (no double lighting from the two real-time lights; also the cheapest shader on phones); metals stay `MeshStandardMaterial`. Draw calls in the counter view fell from 66–100 to 27–33. Previews are JPEG (~45 KB each) to keep the repo small. The grey box stays as a fallback when no bake exists (`?nobake=1` forces it). |

| Nov 5, 2026 | **Head look at the counter (M3):** the eye stays fixed; a one-finger / mouse drag on the 3D view turns the head, camera-style (drag right = turn right), 0.3° per CSS px with light inertia (0.11 s decay), full 360° yaw, pitch −55°…+50°. A one-time "Drag to look around" hint shows on the empty counter (seen-flag in localStorage, a per-device convenience). Camera-feel constants live in `src/render/camera.ts` and `src/input/look.ts`, not tuning.ts. `?look=<yawDeg>,<pitchDeg>` sets the start offset for review. | Nov 5, 2026 | **Customers are 3D: the Microsoft Rocketbox cast (Plan A).** After the 1-day test Colby couldn't tell MakeHuman and Rocketbox apart on the phone and picked Rocketbox: it ships a breathing idle, is 2.5× lighter (7k vs 19k triangles) and has ~115 ready-made everyday people. The older `reputation.general` (points; walkouts −1) stays as it was and still feeds buyer chance. Save v4 adds `youth` and `stats.itemsStolen`. |
| Nov 6, 2026 | **What moves each group (M4):** judged against the item's real worth. *Neighbourhood:* a fair buy (≥ 50% of value) or loan (≥ 30%) for a vulnerable seller (desperation ≥ 0.65, or a pensioner) +0.02; lowballing one (buy < 25%, loan < 12%) −0.03; any insulting offer −0.02; someone giving up in the queue −0.004; a pawner redeeming +0.01. Fakes and frankensteins never count as "fair" (their worth says nothing about how you treated the seller). *Youth:* the same test for students (+0.04 / −0.05), insulting a student −0.05, a student walking out −0.02, selling to a student +0.01. *Specialists:* the mean relationship of every specialist ever retained (grows with each month paid, drops on release), synced after retain/release and at closing. *Police, mob:* untouched until M5. Walkouts were −0.01 in the first pass; the sim's careful shop loses ~2 people a day to the queue, which pinned the street at −0.5…−1 within two months, so it is small now: a slow counter alone drifts to "cool". |
| Nov 6, 2026 | **What each group does (M4), small and tunable:** neighbourhood → buyer chance × (1 ± 0.15) and queue patience × (1 ± 0.2) at the extremes; youth → shoplifting × (1 − 0.6 × youth); specialists, police and mob do nothing yet (standing for the endings; M5 reads police and mob). The ledger shows a "Word on the street" section: one vague line per group (neighbourhood and kids always, specialists once you've retained one, police and mob only once they move), picked by band (cold ≤ −0.5, cool, neutral, warm ≥ 0.15, close ≥ 0.5) or, when today moved a group by ≥ 0.03, by direction ("The neighbourhood is warming to you."). Never numbers. Lines in `src/data/reputationLines.ts`. |
| Nov 6, 2026 | **Shoplifting (M4):** rolled once per open day at closing, from day 8 (nobody has cased a new shop in its first week; the first week is for learning the counter), one seeded draw per displayed item from a stream forked off the day's RNG (`theft:<day>`). Chance = 0.016 × exposure (counter case 0, wall 1, back shelf 0.6, any area a case protects 0) × cheapness (1 at ≤ 4,000 ₽ of real worth, 0 at ≥ 15,000) × foot traffic (people through the door / 10, clamped 0.5–1.5) × youth factor × security factor 1 / (1 + 0.35 × shop.security), capped at 0.25. A stolen item leaves stock, display, tags and `paid` with a GONE ledger line ("The Zenit-E is gone from the wall shelf."), the closing narration, a Stolen row in the day summary and `stats.itemsStolen`. When security stopped a draw that would otherwise have been a theft, the strongest deterrent you own gets one SEEN line that night, so security is felt. 8 cheap things split between wall and back with no security: one gone every ~10 days; with mirror + camera + wall case: about one in 60 days. |
| Nov 6, 2026 | **Security upgrades (M4), in the Office's new "Cases and security" section** (with the better counter case, and a line saying how many things sit on open shelves tonight): door chime 1,200 ₽ (security 0.5, theft ×0.85), convex mirror 2,500 ₽ (security 1, ×0.74), lockable wall case 14,000 ₽ (thieves can't reach the wall; wall buyer visibility ×0.9 because buyers have to ask), security camera 22,000 ₽ (security 3, ×0.49; `effects.records` is stored so M5 can use the tape as evidence). The better counter case keeps its security 1. No prerequisites. |
| Nov 6, 2026 | **M4 sim check (`npm run sim`, reputation and shoplifting in):** careful 12.1%/month over 20 seeds (18.5% before M4; over 100 seeds 17.8% vs 17.9% before, so no systematic change), ~3.3 items stolen per 60 days (it never buys security and fills the wall once the counter is full); careless still busts in 20/20 (median day 29), ~0.1 stolen (it dumps stock in 3 days). ## Parked (do not build without a decision)

- Customer body language: Rocketbox also has nervous, angry, impatient and look-around idles. Play one per temperament or mood as the GDD's posture tells (haggling, M4 people pass); each clip adds ~100 KB per cast member unless shared as a separate animation file.
- Prop texture atlas (all prop base-colour maps in one texture, one material): cuts ~60 draw calls to 1. The quality-tiers measurement (Nov 6) found at most ~43 calls in view, so not needed unless the phone says otherwise.
- Phone landscape: the dialogue strip covers most of the counter. Revisit in the M6 desktop/landscape layout pass.
- Loan extensions/renewals (pay interest only), reputation effects of forfeits (lowballing pawners landed with M4 reputation by group), regulars who re-pawn the same item (M4 Student's Laptop).
- Selling state awards (militaria) is a legal grey area: a hook for the M5 crime layer. Militaria heirloom provenance needs `tuning.items.heirloomCategories`.
- Displayed items rendered in the 3D cases (M3 art pass); the item a buyer wants already shows on the counter mat.
- Customers who walk out remember the price they were offered and come back later with it (GDD §4). Needs a perceived-value field on the haggle record; belongs with regulars (M4), or with M2 walk-in buyers if it's cheap.
- Neighbourhood reputation changing how many people come in a day (GDD § Core loop: "8–14 customers depending on reputation"); waits on timing a real day on the phone (`customersPerDay` stays 8 until then).
- Security camera evidence (M5): the tape could identify a thief, answer Volkova, or prove a seller was the one who brought a stolen piece. Only the `records` flag is stored now.
- Shoplifting extras: a regular kid who is the thief (story hook), getting a stolen item back, theft from the counter case after a mob brick through the glass (M5 vandalism).


- Canal view extras: seasonal variants (snow on the railing, ice, autumn leaves), a dusk/night render with lit windows and lamps crossfaded by the clock, passing boats and pedestrians as small sprites, rain on the glass.

- Walking around the shop or the street.
- A second shop location.
- Multiplayer or shared leaderboards.
- Voice acting.
- Live-service seasonal content.
- AI-generated dialogue at runtime (no API calls in this project).
- Steam / paid desktop build (game is free forever; revisit only if that changes).
